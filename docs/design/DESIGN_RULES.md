## Critical guardrails

Do not interpret anti-generic or anti-AI-slop guidance as a requirement for flat, minimal, visually restrained UI.

Veza design must balance semantic discipline with authored visual quality.

Required principles:

1. Visual hierarchy must use more than whitespace alone.
2. Use contrast deliberately across tone, scale, density, media, and spatial depth.
3. Depth may use tonal fields, controlled overlap, selective elevation, media planes, borders, and section backgrounds.
4. Cards are allowed for meaningful independent units.
5. Bento is allowed for genuinely heterogeneous content with different importance and spans.
6. Public and learner surfaces should be more expressive than governance and administration.
7. Operational surfaces should remain precise and dense without becoming monotonous.
8. Brand colour should be purposeful but visibly present.
9. Imagery, charts, illustration, and product media should participate in the composition when relevant.
10. Motion should communicate state, continuity, product behavior, or narrative.
11. Do not simplify an approved reference into a flatter generic implementation.
12. A usable but bland screen is not complete.

Do not use a reduction-only design process. Removing unnecessary decoration is only one step. The remaining interface must still be composed intentionally.

Read the full root `DESIGN.md` before major UI work.
